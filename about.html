Why is my air conditioner not cooling effectively?

How often should I replace the air filter in my AC?

Air filters should be checked monthly and replaced if dirty or clogged. During peak summer, replacing filters every 1-2 months ensures efficient AC performance.

Is regular AC maintenance necessary?

Yes, annual maintenance is essential to keep your AC efficient. Regular checks help prevent major issues and extend the life of your unit.

Can I service my AC myself?

While you can clean or replace filters, most AC repairs require professional expertise. DIY repairs may cause damage or void warranties. Always hire a licensed technician for major servicing.
