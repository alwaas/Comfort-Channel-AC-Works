Major AC Repair Issues | AC Service Repair In Ambedkar Nagar

  • Lack of Cooling: AC may not cool properly due to dirty or clogged filters, low refrigerant levels, malfunctioning compressor, or thermostat issues.
  • Leaking Water: Water leakage could be caused by a clogged drain line, frozen evaporator coil, or damaged condensate pump.
  • Strange Noises: Unusual sounds like rattling, buzzing, or squealing may indicate issues with the fan motor, compressor, or loose components.
  • Electrical Issues: Check for blown fuses or tripped circuit breakers. Power issues can stop the indoor unit from working.
  • Blocked Air Filters: Dirty or blocked filters can restrict airflow and stop the AC from functioning. Clean or replace filters regularly.
  • Buzzing or Humming: Slight buzzing is normal, but loud or continuous buzzing may indicate a problem.
  • Rattling or Vibration: Check the outdoor unit for loose or damaged parts if you feel shaking or hear rattling.
  • To fix these issues, start by cleaning or changing filters, removing debris around the outdoor unit, and ensuring all components are secure. If problems persist, call Comfort Channel AC Works for professional repair before it worsens.
